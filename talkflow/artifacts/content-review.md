# Can You Trust the Stars?

## 제목

- EN: Can You Trust the Stars?
- KO: 별점을 믿을 수 있을까?

## Topic Hook

- EN: You are choosing a restaurant you have never visited. One place has thousands of five-star reviews, while another has only twenty detailed reviews. Some comments sound genuine, but others feel copied. You have ten minutes to decide.
- KO: 처음 가는 식당을 고르고 있습니다. 한 곳은 별점 5점 리뷰가 수천 개이고, 다른 곳은 자세한 리뷰가 스무 개뿐입니다. 어떤 댓글은 진짜 같지만 다른 댓글은 복사한 것처럼 보입니다. 결정할 시간은 10분입니다.

## Today's Goal

- EN: Explain what makes online information feel trustworthy and make a practical choice.
- KO: 온라인 정보가 믿을 만하다고 느끼는 기준을 설명하고 현실적인 선택을 해 봅니다.

### 1. What is the last thing you checked reviews for?

- Starter: 
- Follow-up: —

### 2. Which do you check first: stars, photos, or comments?

- KO: 별점, 사진, 댓글 중 무엇을 먼저 확인하나요?
- Starter: 
- Follow-up: —

### 3. Would you trust a review with no photo?

- KO: 사진 없는 리뷰를 믿을 수 있나요?
- Starter: 
- Follow-up: —

## Quick Activity

- Type: ranking
- Title EN/KO: Trust Signal Ranking / 신뢰 신호 순위 정하기
- Instruction EN: Rank these from most to least useful. Give one reason for your top choice.
- Instruction KO: 가장 유용한 것부터 순위를 정하고 1위의 이유를 말하세요.
- Options: Recent reviews · Customer photos · Detailed negatives · Verified purchase · Reviewer history

## Easy Entry

### 1. How do you usually read reviews?

- KO: 보통 리뷰를 어떻게 읽나요?
- Starter: I usually start with...
- Follow-up: What do you check next?

### 3. Would you choose a lower-rated place?

- KO: 평점이 더 낮은 곳을 고를 수 있나요?
- Starter: I might choose it if...
- Follow-up: What extra information would help?

## Main Discussion

### 1. What makes a review feel trustworthy to you?

- KO: 어떤 리뷰가 신뢰할 만하게 느껴지나요?
- Starter: I trust a review more when...
- Example Follow-up: Can you remember a review that changed your mind?
- Deeper Follow-up: Can many similar reviews create false trust?

### 2. Should platforms remove reviews they suspect are fake?

- KO: 플랫폼은 가짜로 의심되는 리뷰를 삭제해야 할까요?
- Starter: Platforms should...
- Example Follow-up: What evidence should they require?
- Deeper Follow-up: How can they avoid deleting honest opinions?

### 3. Are negative reviews more useful than positive ones?

- KO: 부정적인 리뷰가 긍정적인 리뷰보다 더 유용한가요?
- Starter: Negative reviews are useful because...
- Example Follow-up: What kind of complaint helps you decide?
- Deeper Follow-up: Can negativity make a good product look unfairly bad?

### 4. Who is responsible when a review leads to a bad choice?

- KO: 리뷰 때문에 잘못 선택했을 때 책임은 누구에게 있을까요?
- Starter: The main responsibility belongs to...
- Example Follow-up: What should the customer check personally?
- Deeper Follow-up: What could make responsibility shared?

## Mid-game

- Type: detective
- Title EN/KO: Review Detective / 리뷰 탐정
- Instruction EN: Choose the two most believable comments and identify the detail that earns your trust.
- Instruction KO: 가장 믿을 만한 댓글 두 개를 고르고 신뢰를 주는 세부 정보를 찾으세요.
- Options: Amazing! Best ever! · The soup was hot, but the wait was 25 minutes. · Five stars. Five stars. Five stars. · I visited at lunch; the set menu was filling, though the tables were close. · Not good. · Staff solved our order mistake quickly.

## Useful Phrases

1. **I take that rating with a grain of salt.**
   - KO: 그 평점은 어느 정도 걸러서 봐요.
   - Usage: When a score may be misleading
2. **The details make it sound credible.**
   - KO: 세부 내용 때문에 믿을 만하게 들려요.
   - Usage: When a review includes specific evidence
3. **That would be a deal-breaker for me.**
   - KO: 그건 저라면 선택하지 않을 결정적 이유예요.
   - Usage: When one problem changes your decision

## Final Round

- EN: Name one review clue you will pay more attention to next time.
- KO: 다음부터 더 주의 깊게 볼 리뷰의 단서 한 가지를 말해 보세요.
- Starter: Next time, I’ll pay more attention to...

## Leader Notes

- Estimated minutes: 100
- Sensitive warning: Avoid pressuring anyone to share private experiences.
- When conversation stops: Can you give a simple example? · Which option feels closest to your view? · Would the answer change in a different situation?
- Recommended skip: Skip the final main question if the activity creates a strong discussion.
- Common notes: Give each person thinking time before follow-ups. · Use the starter as support, not as a required script.

## Quick Activity

- Type: ranking
- Title EN/KO: Keep or Remove / 유지할까, 없앨까
- Instruction EN: Choose one feature to keep and one to remove for a week. Explain both choices.
- Instruction KO: 일주일 동안 유지할 기능 하나와 없앨 기능 하나를 고르고 두 선택의 이유를 말하세요.
- Options: Notifications · Infinite scroll · Read receipts · Autoplay · Screen-time report

## Easy Entry

### 1. When is your phone most helpful?

- KO: 스마트폰이 가장 도움이 되는 때는 언제인가요?
- Starter: My phone is most helpful when...
- Follow-up: What would be difficult without it?

### 2. What small phone habit interrupts you?

- KO: 어떤 작은 스마트폰 습관이 집중을 방해하나요?
- Starter: I get interrupted when...
- Follow-up: Could one setting help?

### 3. Where would you like a phone-free moment?

- KO: 어디에서 스마트폰 없는 시간을 갖고 싶나요?
- Starter: I’d like to put my phone away during...
- Follow-up: What might make that hard?

## Main Discussion

### 1. Are phone habits mainly personal choices or design results?

- KO: 스마트폰 습관은 개인 선택일까요, 설계의 결과일까요?
- Starter: They are influenced more by...
- Example Follow-up: Which feature shapes behavior most?
- Deeper Follow-up: How should responsibility be shared?

### 2. Should useful apps also protect our attention?

- KO: 유용한 앱이 사용자의 주의력도 보호해야 할까요?
- Starter: Apps should/shouldn’t...
- Example Follow-up: What protection would still respect choice?
- Deeper Follow-up: Could protection become unwanted control?

### 3. Is screen time a good measure of healthy use?

- KO: 스크린 타임이 건강한 사용의 좋은 기준일까요?
- Starter: Screen time is useful because...
- Example Follow-up: What kind of use should count differently?
- Deeper Follow-up: What better measure could include purpose and feeling?

### 4. What is a realistic boundary that people can maintain?

- KO: 사람들이 지속할 수 있는 현실적인 경계는 무엇일까요?
- Starter: A realistic boundary is...
- Example Follow-up: What trigger would remind you?
- Deeper Follow-up: When should breaking the rule be completely acceptable?

## Mid-game

- Type: design
- Title EN/KO: Attention Designer / 주의력 디자이너
- Instruction EN: Redesign a phone home screen using only five items. Explain what you deliberately left out.
- Instruction KO: 다섯 항목만 사용해 스마트폰 홈 화면을 다시 구성하고 일부러 뺀 것을 설명하세요.
- Options: Calls · Messages · Camera · Maps · Music · Social media · Browser · Calendar · Weather

## Useful Phrases

1. **I opened it out of habit.**
   - KO: 습관적으로 그 앱을 열었어요.
   - Usage: When an action was automatic
2. **That feature pulls my attention away.**
   - KO: 그 기능이 제 주의를 빼앗아요.
   - Usage: When describing distraction
3. **I need a boundary I can actually maintain.**
   - KO: 실제로 지킬 수 있는 경계가 필요해요.
   - Usage: When rejecting an extreme solution

## Leader Notes

- Estimated minutes: 100
- Sensitive warning: Avoid pressuring anyone to share private experiences.
- When conversation stops: Can you give a simple example? · Which option feels closest to your view? · Would the answer change in a different situation?
- Recommended skip: Skip the final main question if the activity creates a strong discussion.
- Common notes: Give each person thinking time before follow-ups. · Use the starter as support, not as a required script.
